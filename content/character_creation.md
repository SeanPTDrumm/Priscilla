# Character Creation

## Level 3
Use **D&D 2024 rules**. Ability scores use **Standard Array or Point Buy**.

## You are an outsider
Your character has recently arrived from overseas. Have some sense of why they came to Teralis—adventure, exile, following someone, searching for something, escaping obligations, curiosity, starting over, or whatever makes sense to you.

## Backstory
This should not feel like homework. Loose concepts are welcome. We can build details together.

## Quirks
Quirks are the little things that make a character feel like a person: habits, preferences, superstitions, mannerisms, beliefs, physical oddities, or other small details. They have no significant mechanical bonus or penalty.

Before receiving the character's special starting item, record **at least two locked-in Quirks somewhere discoverable on the D&D Beyond character sheet**. You can develop more naturally during play.

## A little something extra
Every character begins with **one special magic item selected with the DM**. You can pitch an idea, or the DM can design it. The former option to decline the item for 50 gp is retired.

Your item is **private to you and the DM** unless you later choose to reveal it in play.

## Optional: Disadvantage for Bonus Feat
You may take **one meaningful mechanical Disadvantage** in exchange for **one additional Feat**. See the current Disadvantages handout in this portal.
